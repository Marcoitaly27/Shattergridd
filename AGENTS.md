# SHATTERGRID project direction

Before design, architectural or implementation work, read
[the user’s Master Game Vision](docs/MASTER_GAME_VISION.md) in full.
It is the intended product and creative direction, not an implementation backlog
or a claim that planned features already exist. Later explicit user instructions
can revise it.

Inspect the actual repository and preserve working systems. Implement only the
requested scope. Report conflicts with the vision rather than silently changing
its principles. Do not invent final characters or implement future systems simply
because they appear in the vision.

Preserve the Law of Reflection unless explicitly changed by the user. Keep
characters data-driven, visuals replaceable, and gameplay independent from art.
Retain flexibility for Core, Guard, Hunter, Power, Special and the provisionally
named Random family, board size and deployment formations.

Prioritize stability, tactical clarity, character identity, gameplay depth and
capture quality before additional content. Unlocks should primarily offer
sidegrades with strengths, weaknesses and counterplay. Keep realistic stone
statues, restrained dark medieval mythology, mystery and short attacker-led
cinematic captures central to the game’s identity.
